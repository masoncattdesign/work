Friday 18 September Illustration Builder

Two ways to build, and the parts to build with

  • Tertiary shapes became containers for System Icons, so any of the 2,891 icons can sit inside a shape rather than only beside one.
  • Templates moved into the right rail next to Shapes and the canvas became clickable, so placing a part is one click instead of a form.
  • The palette stops at nine now, and grey is the default for the largest shape. Both measured off the real artwork rather than picked.
  • Added a second build mode: assemble by hand, or describe what you want in a sentence and let it lay out.
  • Generated the base shapes, with size as a setting rather than a separate shape, and added a fit rule so a secondary shape has to sit inside its base.
  • Built a corpus generator and a real training set from the pilot exports, 16 assets at 512 with captions.
  • Found the depth style pieces fall apart at 64, the keyboards, laptops and news card especially. That is what later drove the small variant work.
  • Put up a showcase page with every asset split across the light and dark surfaces, and added the generated asset studio with a validator that checks the actual pixels: contrast on both surfaces, what gets lost at 64, and how far colors sit from the tokens.

Also stood up builder-windowsai, a separate studio for generated assets.

  • Corrected the Windows preset against what the artwork actually says. 72 shipped files carry 238 distinct colors, and the top 20 are each used by exactly one collection, so the old preset was a guess.
  • Windows and Flat Outline became two real materials measured off the Figma frame, rather than approximations. Windows is rendered with gradients and a two pass cast shadow and no line at all; Flat Outline is white surfaces with one hairline.
  • The accent picker took the Customizer's shape and its seed colors, so the two tools now behave the same way when you pick a color.
  • Devices get drawn from the Figma asset, emblems get a style and a shape, and glyph weight moved into the picker where the glyphs are.
  • Collapsed four modes down to one. Two of them were a preset and a glyph source wearing mode costumes, and a device turned out to be a way of looking rather than a mode.
  • Started the pieces underneath all of it: a parts library, a recipe format, the generator and the first Windows builder.
  • Wrote down how we treat the M365 system, which is that it influences the direction without dictating it, plus a reference entry for the Fluent illustration style.

Wednesday 9 September Bridge · Docs

Ada's color contract, read against our tools

  • Ada sent over the Bridge color scheme handoff. Read it through and mapped it against the Customizer.
  • Two words collide: Bridge uses role and archetype to mean specific things, and our tools use the same two words for different ones. Worth settling before the two systems talk to each other.
  • Good news is that plate, glyph and accent are legal Bridge part names as is, so the anatomy work does not need anyone's approval.

Friday 4 September App Icons · Customizer

46 more app icons, and this time they are drawn at every size

  • Imported 46 Windows app icons at 16, 24, 32 and 48. These are authored at each size rather than scaled from one tile, so not one of them carries a generated flag. App Icons is 78 assets now.
  • Four retired apps came in as deprecated rather than published, each pointing at what replaces it. First time anything in the library has used that status.
  • 27 frames on the board were skipped and each says why, so the next person does not re-litigate it. Store nearly slipped through as a duplicate under a different slug.
  • Customizer picked up the color roles pass from the call with Ada. Parts are named for what they do now, so a shape is a plate, a glyph or an accent rather than named after wherever it came from.
  • Grouping runs on chromaticity instead of full color distance, which is what makes a group come out as "these are the purples" rather than splitting light from dark. Intentional white and black are held out rather than themed, and anything drawn on top of a shape has stopped counting as part of it.
  • One canvas size across the board, so an export is one set of files instead of a set per size.

Library at 3,280 assets and 15,470 drawings.
