Friday 4 September App Icons

46 more app icons, and this time they are drawn at every size

  • Imported 46 Windows app icons at 16, 24, 32 and 48. These are authored at each size rather than scaled from one tile, so not one of them carries a generated flag. App Icons is 78 assets now.
  • Four retired apps came in as deprecated rather than published, each pointing at what replaces it. First time anything in the library has used that status.
  • 27 frames on the board were skipped and each says why, so the next person does not re-litigate it. Store nearly slipped through as a duplicate under a different slug.

Library at 3,280 assets and 15,470 drawings.

Thursday 3 September Work site · Docs

The work page got its own site, and the documents stopped remembering their numbers

  • Moved this site out of the Expressive Assets repo. Sharing that URL and navigation made a view of everything read as one more page about icons.
  • About and the System Map now read their counts from manifest.json. They were claiming 3,129 assets in 6 collections against a library holding 3,280 in 8, and one tile read "0 published" directly below a paragraph saying everything is published.
  • Deleted the derived Outline and Filled from App Icons: 384 drawings, none of them drawn. A full matrix filled by derivation claims something the artwork does not support.

A stale count now fails CI rather than sitting there looking finished.

Wednesday 2 September Anatomy · Tooling

Asset Anatomy stops describing the artwork and starts showing it

  • The anatomy figures are generated from the real shipped SVG rather than redrawn, so a figure cannot drift from the library it is documenting.
  • Renamed the badge part to emblem, because badge already means something else in Windows and a vocabulary that fights the platform will lose.
  • Priced the naming decision against Bridge's own rule instead of guessing at it, and wrote a probe for whether the REST API returns the plugin data the Figma round trip is designed around. That assumption had never been tested.
  • Every product, app and third-party icon now has all six sizes.
Tuesday 1 September 2.0 · Documents

Version 2.0, and the library gets documents to argue with

  • Contract break: group entries carry an id and no longer map onto type, and placeholders became generated. Anyone reading manifest.json has to notice.
  • 61 third-party brand marks and 32 Windows app tiles imported. M365 Illustrations replaced: 28 at 160px out, 40 at 512px in.
  • File Icons became their own group and lost Filled. What shipped as Filled was derived rather than drawn, and nothing upstream has one, so 799 drawings went.
  • About, System Map and Asset Anatomy landed as one set, and everything received is published.

Aligned to Bridge, and the dark theme kept on purpose. Checking artwork against both grounds is the job here, not a preference.

Monday 31 August Library

The product icons come from the Figma library, and the tools get their names

  • Replaced the product icons with the real Figma library: 46 assets became 90.
  • M365 Illustrations added, with Outline and Filled derived and a recolor that respects the brand palette rather than flattening it.
  • The browsing tool is Gallery and the styling tool is Customizer. Two tools with plain names beat one tool with a clever one.
